Comeback Kids

Names that were dominant before 1975 — sometimes given to tens of thousands of babies a year — fell to near-zero, and are now climbing back. Emma is the prototype: practically extinct in the 1970s, #1 by 2008.

nobodynamed is a small data project. Names are forever. Mostly.
