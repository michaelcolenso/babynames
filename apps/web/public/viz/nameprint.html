Visualization 33 / The identity record

The Nameprint

Every name leaves a fingerprint in time. Each ridge below is one year of American births—pressure, repetition, disappearance, return. No two names make the same mark.

The outline is history, not handwriting. Radius encodes annual births on a logarithmic scale; asymmetry encodes acceleration and decline. A second-sex record, when present, burns through in ember.
