# Press Start to Name

In January 2022, the game *Pokémon Legends: Arceus* shipped. That same year, for the first time in American history, the Social Security Administration recorded seven baby boys named **Arceus** — after the Pokémon universe's literal creator-god. The name had never existed before. The release date *is* the birth date.

That's the pattern, over and over: a character lands, and within a year the name lands too. Take every distinctive video-game name and check the birth records, and you get a roster of fandom leaking into the nursery. Pick a fighter.

## Final Fantasy is the MVP

No franchise has minted more real names — and one is a beautiful deep cut. **Aeris** appears in 1998, right after Final Fantasy VII's US release; **Aerith** — the *corrected* localization spelling — shows up later and surges with the 2020 Remake. The translation history of a single character is preserved in two separate waves of babies. Add **Tidus**, **Auron**, **Rinoa**, **Noctis**, **Sephiroth**, **Tifa**, and **Vaan**, each arriving within a year of its game.

## Named the year the game shipped

The Arceus effect is everywhere once you look. **Ezreal** first appears in 2010, the year the League of Legends champion launched. **Yennefer** debuts in 2020, the year after Netflix's *Witcher*. Assassin's Creed even names one child per release — **Altair**, then **Ezio**, then **Bayek**, then **Eivor** — and Mortal Kombat coined **Kitana** and **Mileena** from nothing in 1994, dead-on the arcade-and-movie craze.

## The heavyweight: a game resurrected a flapper name

The biggest name on the board isn't a coinage — it's a rescue. **Zelda** belonged to Zelda Fitzgerald and was nearly extinct by mid-century, then *Breath of the Wild* (2017) brought it to an all-time peak. A video game didn't just add a name; it brought a 1920s one back from the dead.

## The impostors

Honesty check: some names *look* like a gamer's pick but aren't. **Mario**, **Dante**, and **Lara** are older than their games; **Jinx** peaked in 1950, not with *Arcane*; **Zidane** is the footballer, not Final Fantasy IX.
