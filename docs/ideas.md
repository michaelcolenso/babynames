Fun one — the "debut of the year" almanac: for each year, the boy name that **arrived with the biggest splash** (highest count in its very first year on record). Pulling all of it:

This list is *spectacular* — it's basically a century of American media history written in birth certificates. Almost every entry has a clear trigger. Decoded:

## War heroes & headlines (1910s–40s)

- **1918 · Foch** (58) — Marshal Ferdinand Foch, supreme Allied commander as WWI ended
- **1916 · Verdun** (14) — the battle, mid-WWI
- **1931 · Rockne** (17) — Knute Rockne, the Notre Dame coach, killed in a plane crash that March
- **1935 · Haile** (11) — Haile Selassie, *Time*'s Man of the Year
- **1940 · Willkie** (13) — Wendell Willkie, that year's GOP nominee
- **1942 · Mcarther** (23) — Gen. Douglas MacArthur (phonetic spelling)
- **1949 · Ezzard** (21) — Ezzard Charles, the new heavyweight champ

## The TV-western & idol era (1950s–60s)

- **1957 · Maverick** (33) — the western premiered that September (it also gave us *Bret*)
- **1959 · Rowdy** (22) — Rowdy Yates, Clint Eastwood's role on *Rawhide*
- **1965 · Illya** (35) — Illya Kuryakin, the teen-idol spy of *The Man from U.N.C.L.E.*
- **1963 · Medgar** (25) — Medgar Evers, the civil-rights leader assassinated that June

## The music & screen wave (1970s–2010s) — where it gets loud

- **1974 · Nakia** (**613**) — the ABC series *Nakia*; the single biggest boy debut on the whole list
- **1977 · Levar** (**523**) — LeVar Burton, the year *Roots* aired
- **1972 · Jabbar** (76) — Kareem Abdul-Jabbar
- **1994 · Shyheim** (168) — the teenage Wu-Tang-affiliated rapper
- **2001 · Jahiem** (155) — R&B's Jaheim · **2003 · Pharrell** (67) · **2004 · Jkwon** (100, "Tipsy") · **2005 · Jayceon** (49, = The Game) · **2009 · Jeremih** (88)
- **2011 · Jionni** (63) — Snooki's beau on *Jersey Shore*
- **2014 · Llewyn** (38) — the Coens' *Inside Llewyn Davis* · **2015 · Gotham** (48, the TV series)
- **2017 · Asahd** (59) — DJ Khaled's toddler-turned-brand · **2018 · Jahseh** (65) — XXXTentacion's real name, the year he died

## A telenovela/Latino thread runs through the 2000s–10s

**2007 · Yurem** (206), **2008 · Yosgart** (72), **2010 · Vadhir** (55) — child stars and telenovela leads driving Spanish-language debuts.

## And our old friend, the data bug

**1989 · "Christop"** (1,082) — not a name at all. That's the **8-character truncation artifact** we caught earlier (Christopher with its tail cut off), and it's so large it wins 1989 outright. A nice involuntary cameo from the data's own glitch.
