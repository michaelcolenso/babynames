## One sound, 59 spellings

The family is 59 names. 22 end in -ayden, 18 in -aden, 13 in -aiden, and 6 in -aeden.

Together they account for 1,506,945 boys since 1880.

The sound is older than the boom. 6 of the spellings were already in the record before 1920: Aden, Hayden, Graden, Haden, Braden, Grayden. Hayden and Aden are still on birth certificates today.

What changed was not the sound. It was the number of parents willing to use it.

## The curve

In 1970, the whole family took 148 boys. In 1990, 1,486. In 2000, 17,460.

Then it went vertical. The peak came in 2008: 87,994 boys, and all 59 spellings recorded births that year.

## One year, two winners

[Jayden](/name/Jayden/) has the biggest single peak in the family: 17,316 boys in 2009.

[Aiden](/name/Aiden/) peaked in the same year, at 16,030. By 2025 the order reversed. Aiden took 5,046 boys. Jayden took 4,653.

Jayden won the peak. Aiden won the present.

## The same mountain, moved

30 of the 59 peaked in the 2000s. 24 peaked in the 2010s. 4 peaked in the 2020s.

Of the 59, 1 peaked before 2000. [Paden](/name/Paden/) reached 78 boys in 1987 and never came close again.

[Brayden](/name/Brayden/) peaked at 9,122 in 2010. [Kayden](/name/Kayden/) peaked at 4,742 in 2014. In 2025 they took 1,545 and 2,678 boys.
