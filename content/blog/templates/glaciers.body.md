## Why it matters

A flash flood name — Kunta, Moesha, Jkwon — is a timestamp. You can read the exact year of the TV premiere off the chart.

A glacier is a worldview. It takes two or three generations of parents, each one nudging the same name a little further up or down, to build one. Nobody chose Robert; everybody chose Robert. These are the names that shaped what America sounded like, one percent at a time — and they are also the reason your coworker's father is one of five Daves in the office.

Every glacier here has its own vital record — living population, median age, year-by-year counts — on its name page. Start with [Robert](/name/Robert/) or [Mary](/name/Mary/), or see the flash floods that did the opposite in a tenth of the time.
