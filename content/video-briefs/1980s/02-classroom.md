# Video brief 02 — Classroom (1984)

## Hook (the no-repeat twist)

"This is what an average American classroom sounded like in 1984 — thirty kids,
thirty *different* names. Even Michael and Jennifer couldn't guarantee a
duplicate."

## Script (15–30 seconds, ~70 words)

"We rebuilt an average 30-kid classroom from the actual 1984 birth records —
14 girls, 16 boys, seats dealt by the real national distribution. The twist:
no name repeats. Not Jennifer. Not Michael. Michael was the decade's biggest
name — 663,807 boys — and his expected share of a class was just 0.60 seats.
The blockbuster-name era was already more varied than memory says. Full roster
on NobodyNamed."

## Shot list

1. **0–3s** — Screen recording: `/names/1980s/classroom/` roster grid,
   slow pan over the 30 seat cards.
2. **3–10s** — Rapid roll-call cuts, one seat card per beat (girls): Amanda,
   Amy, Ashley, Crystal, Elizabeth, Heather, Jennifer, Jessica, Megan, Melissa,
   Nicole, Sarah, Stephanie, Tiffany.
3. **10–16s** — Roll call continues (boys): Andrew, Brandon, Christopher,
   Daniel, David, James, Jason, John, Joseph, Joshua, Justin, Matthew, Michael,
   Robert, Ryan, William.
4. **16–22s** — Graphic: "30 seats · 30 names · 0 repeats". Stat callout:
   "Michael's expected seats: 0.60 of 16 boys' seats."
5. **22–end** — Methodology disclaimer card (see on-screen text) + URL.
